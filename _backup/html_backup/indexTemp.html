How any SME grows, step by step.

The same simple path works for any small business. Typical pacing is shown, and you approve each move.

1Week 1

Choose who you want to reach

Pick one sector, your ideal customer and a clear offer. The agent drafts your target list, a 0 to 100 scoring guide and the likeliest customer pain points.

2Week 1

Check the list before you trust it

Sample 20 contacts first. If more than about one in five is wrong, or bounces pass 3%, pause and fix the source.

3Weeks 2 to 3

Send a four-step email sequence

Top-scoring contacts get full personalisation. You approve the emails, which go out over about two weeks with an opt-out in each, and the agent drafts replies.

4From week 2

Publish helpful content

Two social posts a week and useful blog articles build trust and bring owners to you, including through Google and AI assistants.

5Always ready

Make it easy to say yes

One simple page, a short form and a booking link straight after. Replying within five minutes sharply improves the chance of a booking.

6After 48 to 72 hours

7Every week

Review and improve
